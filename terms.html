Tokens: how they work

What a token is

A token is the unit in which AI processing is measured — roughly three-quarters of an English word. Your balance is debited only when you invoke an AI feature. Reading, scrolling, searching, highlighting, annotating and syncing consume no tokens.

Indicative rates

These figures are estimates, not guarantees. Actual consumption varies with the length and density of the passage, the language, and the model version in use. The app shows the exact debit for every action in the token meter, and the full ledger is exportable from Settings → Tokens → Export usage.

Buying tokens

Tokens are sold two ways:

  • One-time pack — 1,500,000 tokens for US$6.99 (about $4.66 per million). Charged once. No subscription. The balance never expires.
  • Monthly refill — 1,500,000 tokens for US$4.99 per month (about $3.33 per million), a 29% saving. Charged monthly until you cancel. Unused tokens roll over and accumulate for as long as the refill is active.

Expiry, roll-over and forfeiture

  • Purchased tokens — from packs or refills — do not expire while your account is open.
  • If you cancel a monthly refill, the tokens already in your balance remain usable indefinitely; you simply stop receiving new ones.
  • If you delete your account, or we terminate it for a breach under section 15, any unspent balance is forfeited without refund.
  • Tokens have no cash value, cannot be redeemed for money, and cannot be sold, transferred or shared between accounts, except within a Team pool under section 8.

Running out

When your balance reaches zero the AI features pause. Nothing you have already generated is removed, hidden or locked — every past summary, answer and review card stays fully readable, and the reader itself is unaffected. You can top up at any time, or wait for your next refill or monthly free grant.
