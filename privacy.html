The short version

Your PDFs stay on your device. We never upload whole documents, and we never build a copy of your library on our servers.

Only what you ask about is sent. When you request a summary, an explanation or an answer, the relevant passage travels to our AI provider, is processed, and is discarded. That passage is also the only text your tokens are ever spent on.

Nothing you send trains a model. Not ours, not our provider's. This is contractual, not a courtesy.

We sell nothing. No advertising, no data brokers, no third-party trackers in the reader.

The sections below are the full detail. If anything here conflicts with the summary above, the full detail governs.

What we never collect

  • Your documents. PDFs are opened, indexed and rendered locally. No full file is transmitted to us or stored by us.
  • Your highlights, notes and review cards. These live in your local library. If you turn on sync they are encrypted on your device before they leave it, and we hold only ciphertext we cannot read.
  • Your reading behaviour. We do not log which documents you open, which pages you linger on, or how long you read.
  • Advertising identifiers. There are no ad SDKs, no pixels and no cross-site trackers in the application.
  • Precise location. We infer a country from your billing details for tax purposes and nothing finer.

Your documents and the AI

This is the part most people want to understand, so it is worth being exact.

What leaves your device

When you invoke an AI feature, the app assembles a request payload containing only the text needed to answer: the passage or page you selected, a small amount of surrounding context for coherence, and your question if you asked one. Images, embedded files, document metadata and the rest of the file stay put.

What happens to it

The payload is sent over TLS 1.3 to our AI provider, processed to generate your answer, and then discarded. We do not write the payload to our own database. Our provider is contractually barred from retaining it beyond the short abuse-monitoring window described in their terms, and from using it to train or fine-tune any model.

What comes back

The generated summary, explanation, answer or review card is returned to your device and saved into your local library alongside the citation that produced it. We keep no copy.

Local-only mode. If you never use an AI feature, no document text ever leaves your device — the network is used only for sign-in, sync and update checks.

Token and billing data

Because Liqrai charges by the token rather than by the month, we keep a ledger so that both of us can check the arithmetic. Each ledger entry records the time, the feature invoked, and the number of tokens consumed. It does not record the document, the passage, the question or the answer.

You can export your full ledger as CSV from Settings → Tokens → Export usage, and you can see the running total at any moment on the token meter in the app.

Payments are handled by our payment processor. Full card numbers reach them directly and never touch our servers; we receive only a token reference, the card brand and its last four digits.

How we use information

  • To provide the reader, the sync service and the AI companion you asked for.
  • To meter, bill and refill tokens accurately, and to send receipts.
  • To keep the service secure — detecting credential stuffing, abusive automation and token fraud.
  • To fix defects, using crash reports and aggregate performance data.
  • To reply to support requests.
  • To send service messages you cannot opt out of, such as a notice that your refill failed. Marketing email is opt-in and separately unsubscribable.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects, and we do not profile you.

How long we keep it

  • Account data — for as long as your account exists, then 30 days, then deleted.
  • Token ledger — 24 months, so that you can audit past usage and we can answer billing disputes.
  • Invoices — 7 years, because tax law requires it. This period survives account deletion.
  • AI request payloads — not retained by us at all.
  • Crash reports — 90 days.
  • Support correspondence — 24 months after the ticket closes.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to our processing of your personal data, and to receive it in a portable format. We extend these rights to everyone who uses Liqrai, wherever they are.

  • Access and portability — Settings → Account → Download my data produces a JSON archive of your account record and token ledger, usually within minutes.
  • Correction — edit your profile in the app, or write to us.
  • Deletion — Settings → Account → Delete account. This is irreversible, takes effect within 30 days, and forfeits any unspent token balance.
  • Objection and restriction — email us and we will act within 30 days.
  • Withdrawing consent — where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting processing already carried out.

If you believe we have handled your data badly, please tell us first — but you are entitled to complain to your local supervisory authority regardless.

Security

  • TLS 1.3 for everything in transit; AES-256 at rest.
  • End-to-end encryption for synced highlights, notes and review cards, with keys derived on your device and never sent to us.
  • Passwords hashed with Argon2id. Optional two-factor authentication.
  • Least-privilege access for staff, with every access to production logged and reviewed.
  • Annual third-party penetration testing and a published security contact for researchers.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will notify you and the relevant authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.

International transfers

Our processors operate in the United States, the European Union and Ireland, so your data may be transferred outside your country. Where that happens from the EEA or the UK, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses together with a transfer impact assessment, and on the UK International Data Transfer Addendum where applicable.

Children

Liqrai Reader is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. Users between 13 and the age of digital consent in their country need a parent or guardian's permission. If you believe a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We will post any revision here with a new version number and date. For material changes — a new category of data, a new purpose, a new processor handling document text — we will email account holders and show an in-app notice at least 30 days before the change takes effect.
